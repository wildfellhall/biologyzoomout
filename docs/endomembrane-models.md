# Endomembrane animations

The close-up rough ER, smooth ER and Golgi models run automatically under the existing autoplay preference. Reduced-motion users start paused. All support replay and deterministic forward/backward scrubbing. Smooth ER offers lipid assembly and calcium storage in the animation selector.

| Model | Representation | Limits |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Rough ER | A bound ribosome feeds a nascent secretory chain through a translocon; folding precedes export. | Eighteen beads represent a polypeptide, not a functional sequence. Targeting factors, chaperones and coat machinery are omitted. |
| Golgi | Cargo remains within a cisterna as it matures from cis to trans; glycan processing precedes sorting into two destination classes. | A schematic maturation model. Returning enzyme carriers are omitted. Bead counts do not encode a specific glycan. |
| Smooth ER: lipids | Membrane enzymes synthesize lipids on the cytosolic side; new lipids enter one leaflet and a scramblase redistributes them. | Precursor shapes stand for molecular groups. Multiple reactions are condensed. Lipid synthesis is not exclusive to smooth ER. |
| Smooth ER: calcium | Pump-mediated uptake, channel-mediated release, and reuptake of twelve tracer ions. | Counts are conserved but are not concentrations. ATP, signaling molecules and pump stoichiometry are not drawn. Calcium storage is an ER function. |

Cutaways expose luminal cargo without transparent overlapping membranes. Colors identify components, not microscopy staining. Budding, scission and fusion are represented by carriers rather than a membrane-mechanics simulation. Display seconds are not physiological rates. Whole-cell miniatures retain the existing secretion overview and inspection links; no curriculum nodes or biological zoom relationships change.

References: Alberts et al., *Molecular Biology of the Cell*, [The Endoplasmic Reticulum](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK26841/) and [Transport from the ER through the Golgi Apparatus](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK26941/). These links and model qualifications are also available in the in-app model-information panel.

Search indexes the local molecule/model catalogs, pathways, all 67 lessons and all 234 ontology nodes. It needs no remote service, keeps input out of persistent storage, recognizes organelle aliases, and resolves each result to an existing app route. Opening search and dismissing it preserves the current lesson, model, and chooser filters.
