# AP Biology practice authoring blueprint

Prepared before item authoring, 2026-11-21. Course: 2026–27 AP Biology, CED effective Fall 2025 with June 2026 corrections. Original instructional practice; not College Board questions or an exam-score predictor.

## Scope and distribution

- 60 four-option conceptual MCQs, one per framework topic (unit counts 7, 10, 5, 6, 5, 8, 12, 7). Shared between inline concept review and the separate practice bank; repeat exposure is intentional and labeled. Keys remain hidden until an answer is submitted.
- Existing lesson checkpoint questions retain stable answer IDs and gain a fourth misconception-based option. First-attempt records and written recall remain intact.
- Eight original short FRQ exercises, one per unit, each with four separately assessed one-point tasks, a complete stimulus, a custom rubric, worked response, and revision prompt. Emphasis: conceptual analysis, model interpretation, calculations, and evidence-based explanation. No automated written grading.
- Lessons retain their interactive experiments as a separate activity. Assessment language emphasizes concept checks and transfer rather than experimental variables.

## Design before authoring

MCQs ask learners to apply a relationship to a changed context, interpret a representation, or calculate and explain a biological quantity. Each has one best answer and three specific misconceptions with individual feedback. Correct positions rotate evenly across A–D. Topic and authored ontology-node IDs supply the crosswalk; these IDs are not official College Board skill codes. Each item specifies an authored reasoning category. All numeric scenarios are invented teaching examples; all necessary values, units, assumptions, and sequence directions must appear in the stimulus. Calculators are permitted, though the arithmetic is deliberately short.

FRQ sequence by unit: protein structure/function; membrane transport; chemiosmosis; signaling pathway; meiosis; gene expression; allele-frequency change; trophic energy. Each task must be solvable from its local stimulus and course concepts. Rubric criteria must describe the required biological relation or calculation, accept equivalent phrasing, and avoid double penalties. No inference of statistical significance without uncertainty information.

Known gaps: this focused bank does not reproduce exam unit weights or the full six-science-practice distribution. It has no timed mock, long nine-point FRQ, graph-construction scoring, or psychometric calibration. One question per topic does not assess every essential-knowledge statement.

## Persistence and review

Untimed by default. MCQ submissions retain item/version snapshots, selected option, correctness, and correction notes. FRQ text autosaves; requesting feedback snapshots the submitted draft before showing criteria. Later revisions and earlier submitted responses remain available. Item version changes create a fresh record and retain older records. Failed browser storage must be visible to learners. First-attempt accuracy is practice feedback, never an AP 1–5 score.

Validation must independently recompute quantitative keys, check option uniqueness and key balance, confirm every topic is represented, and exercise answer concealment, persistence, revision, keyboard access, narrow screens, and offline access. Structural checks do not establish item difficulty or expert/psychometric validation.

## Extension before stimulus-item authoring

The initial review on 2026-11-21 identified a need for more sustained AP-style reasoning alongside short conceptual retrieval. Add 16 stimulus-based MCQs, two per unit, for 76 total practice MCQs. These use complete original tables, sequences, or pathway representations; emphasize mechanism, quantitative interpretation, and limits of inference; and have option-specific feedback. They do not replace or appear in the 60 inline concept checks. This remains a focused, uncalibrated bank, not a full exam. The separate MCQ card labels distinguish stimulus practice from concept review.
